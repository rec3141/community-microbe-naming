# ScIQ card pictures

One picture per ScIQ card (iq.js `cardImages`: `img/iq/<microbe id>_<card number>.jpg`), drawn from the clue and question on the card's back. Made 2026-10-21 with Google's `gemini-3.1-flash-image` (the first three with `agy`, the rest through OpenRouter as `google/gemini-3.1-flash-image`, 16:9), then resized to 800 px JPEG. No people are shown, to avoid stereotyped depictions of community members.

Style added to every prompt:

> Vibrant charming 2D scientific cartoon illustration, flat colours, soft outlines, warm and respectful, for high-school students, wide 16:10 landscape composition, set around Chesterfield Inlet on Hudson Bay in Nunavut (low rocky tundra, cold sea inlet, tundra lakes, big sky, no trees). Cartoon bacteria have simple friendly faces and no limbs. No people or human figures, no cultural objects or clothing, no buildings. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers or labels.
