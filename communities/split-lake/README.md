# Split Lake (Manitoba)

Second community site, built from the Igluligaarjuk template. Samples: August 2024 survey of
Split Lake, Assean and Clark Lakes and the Burntwood, Nelson, Odie, Grassy and Aiken Rivers
(flowcells FAR93992, FAZ32379). 13 of them are in the freshwater co-assembly
(`freshwater-coassembly-flye-nanoraw`). Not in it: FAR93992 barcodes 13–17 (no reads), TCN
re-runs on FAZ82303 (failed), and the February 2026 community monitoring run FBA07479
(read-level results only, private microscape run `splitlake_fba07479`).

Language: Swampy Cree (N-dialect). Dictionary: the Moose & Swampy Cree Conversation Manual
(atlas-ling.ca / Mushkegowuk Council, 2016, © no open licence), parsed to
`~/split-lake_inputs/dictionary_split-lake.js` (327 entries). The full Swampy Cree dictionary
(dictionary.swampycree.atlas-ling.ca) was down on 2026-11-11. Place-name spellings and live URL: not decided yet.
`env_slug`: `split-lake`.
