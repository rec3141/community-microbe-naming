# Event card pictures (Microbe Clash)

One picture per Event card, shared by every community (`event-<id>.jpg`, see site/cards/engine.js: `e.art`). Made 2026-10-22 through OpenRouter with `google/gemini-3.1-flash-image` (`openrouter_json_images.py`, aspect 16:9), then resized to 600 px JPEG for the 16:9 art box on the card (cards.css). No people, buildings, cultural objects or text.

Regenerated once: `event-freeze` (the first version had written labels, "brine channels" and "protective sparkles"); the second prompt adds "A purely visual picture with no captions, arrows, callouts or written words anywhere."

Style added to every prompt:

> Vibrant charming 2D scientific cartoon illustration, flat colours, soft outlines, warm and respectful, for high-school students, wide 16:9 landscape composition, set around Chesterfield Inlet on Hudson Bay in Nunavut (low rocky tundra, cold sea inlet, tundra lakes, big sky, no trees). Cartoon bacteria have simple friendly faces and no limbs. No people or human figures, no cultural objects or clothing, no buildings. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers or labels.
