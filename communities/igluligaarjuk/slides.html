How Scientific Naming Works

Giving Bacteria Official Inuktitut-Rooted Names with SeqCode

Until recently, bacteria could only be named if grown in a laboratory dish. Now, under the SeqCode (Code of Nomenclature of Prokaryotes Described from Sequence Data), a high-quality DNA genome from nature can serve as the official blueprint!

What Makes a Genome Eligible?

  • High Completeness (≥90%) & Low Contamination (≤5%) so we know the genome belongs to one single organism.
  • Ribosomal & Transfer RNAs (16S, 23S, 5S + 20 tRNAs) that act as the cell's protein-making signature.
  • Unnamed in Global Databases (GTDB): Currently listed only as blank (`s__`) or a computer code (`g__TMED14`, `g__SXXK01`, `sp028352435`).

How We Create a Name Together

  • Every species has two parts: Genus (group name) + species (specific name).
  • We can honor Inuktitut place names (Igluligaarjuk, Itivia), habitats (tariuq = sea, tasiq = lake, siku = sea ice), or what the microbe does!
Where These Microbes Live

Our Sampling Sites Around Chesterfield Inlet (`CDIT`)

🌊 Marine Sites (Inlet, Harbour & Itivia)

  • Itivia (`CDIT_SITE1`, `CDIT4`, `CDIT11`, `CDIT24`): Outer inlet and tidal waters rich in sulfur-oxidizing and solar-powered bacteria.
  • Harbour / Dock (`CDIT_SITE2`, `CDIT12`, `CDIT28`): Coastal harbour where our #1 marine candidate is 83× more abundant than anywhere else in the Arctic!
  • Coastal Waters (`CDIT_SITE3-A, 3-B, 3-D`): Rich in sea-ice algae and kelp-digesting bacteria.

💧 Freshwater Sites (1st, 2nd & 3rd Lake)

  • 1st Lake (`CDIT_SITE3-C`, `CDIT1, 5, 8, 27, 31`): Community drinking water lake, home to pristine organic-matter recyclers (`Polynucleobacter`, `Ferruginibacter`).
  • 2nd Lake (`CDIT2, 6, 9, 25, 30`): Rich in giant-genome Planctomycetes (`QWQI01`, `SXXK01`) that digest moss and lichen sugars.
  • 3rd Lake Area (`CDIT_SITE6`, `CDIT3, 7, 10, 26, 29`): Home to cold-water nitrite-purifying `Nitrotoga` and golden `Aurantibacillus`.

Hands-On Workshop Activity

Let’s Name Chesterfield Inlet’s Bacteria Together!

How we will choose and register the official names in the SeqCode Registry:

Step-by-Step Naming Process

  • 1. Pick a Bacterium: Choose a Marine (Harbour, Itivia) or Freshwater (1st, 2nd, 3rd Lake) candidate that inspires you.
  • 2. Choose an Inuktitut Word or Place Name: It can describe where it lives, what it eats/makes, or its personality.
  • 3. Story & Meaning: Record the exact Inuktitut spelling, pronunciation, and story behind the name for the official publication!

Example Name Patterns

  • Place Name: Polaribacter igluligaarjukensis ("from Igluligaarjuk") or Ferruginibacter itiviensis
  • Habitat / Trait: Tariuqimonas ("sea microbe"), Tasiqisphaera ("lake sphere"), Siqiniqibacter ("sunlight rod")
