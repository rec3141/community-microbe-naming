Style added to every prompt:

> Vibrant charming 2D scientific cartoon illustration, flat colours, soft outlines, friendly and clear for high-school students, square 1:1, a northern Canadian setting (tundra lakes, cold ocean inlet, low hills, no trees), small round and rod-shaped cartoon bacteria with simple happy faces and no limbs. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers or labels.
