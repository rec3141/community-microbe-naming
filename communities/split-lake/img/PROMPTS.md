# Microbe pictures

One square picture per microbe, named in `candidates.js` (`img`). Until a picture exists, use
`placeholder.svg`. Record every picture here: file, MAG id, model used, and the full prompt.

Prompt pattern used for Igluligaarjuk (see `communities/igluligaarjuk/img/PROMPTS.md`):

> Vibrant 2D scientific cartoon illustration, centered circular composition, no text, no
> words, no letters. A single <shape: rod / coccus / curved rod / filament> bacterium
> <colour>, with NO arms, NO legs (purely a cell body), big friendly cartoon eyes and a smile,
> <one visible trait: sulfur globules, gas vesicles, flagellum>, <doing its job: eating
> <food>, releasing <product>>, in <habitat: under lake ice, harbour water, ...> background.

Match the shape to the genome (`shapeGenes` in community.js; rod genes mreB/rodA suggest a rod).
