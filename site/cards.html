⌂ Microbes

The cards

Every microbe from the workshop is a card. Its HP, attacks and weaknesses come straight from its DNA and from where we found it, so each card teaches something true. Tap a card to see it big and find out where its numbers come from.

How to play

2 players · about 15 minutes · one printed set of cards, plus coins or beads for nutrients.

Full rules

Print the cards

Cards print at the standard size of 63 × 88 mm, with thin cut lines along the edges: 9 to a page on A4, 8 to a page (sideways) on Letter. Keep the printer's normal margins and print at 100% scale (or "Actual size"), not "Fit to page".
